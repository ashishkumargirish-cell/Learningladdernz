How I Teach: Step-by-Step Pedagogical Approach

The full Learning Ladder process, one rung at a time.

01

Initial Diagnostic Assessment

I start by evaluating each student's current strengths, learning style, and gaps in a relaxed, encouraging manner without test pressure.

02

Customized 'Ladder' Learning Plan

I design personalized lesson plans aligned with NZ Curriculum achievement objectives, breaking complex topics into small, achievable steps.

03

Concrete-to-Abstract Progression

For younger students, I use concrete objects/manipulatives and visual aids before introducing formal written symbols and equations.

04

Interactive & Scaffolded Guidance

Using the 'I Do, We Do, You Do' framework, I model concepts clearly, guide them through practice together, and then empower independent problem-solving.

05

Positive Reinforcement & Growth Mindset

I actively celebrate progress, normalise mistakes as valuable learning opportunities, and help kids overcome math or reading anxiety.

06

Transparent Parent Communication

I provide regular feedback updates to parents after sessions, keeping them informed of progress, milestones, and quick home-practice tips.
